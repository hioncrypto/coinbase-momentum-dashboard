## The insight

Yachting already has inventory, brokers, and trust circles.  
What it lacks is a **trusted demand layer** — a private way for qualified buyers and charter clients to discover, signal intent, and get matched without public listing noise.

Painted Ladies becomes that layer.

**Not:** another brochure agency.  
**Yes:** the private network where serious capital meets the right yacht — with marketing as the engine, not the product.

## Brand idea: “Invitation over interruption”

**Painted Ladies** = distinctive, curated, impossible to confuse with commodity brokerage media.

Creative posture:

- Provenance stories, not feature dumps
- One perfect introduction > 1,000 impressions

Tagline direction: *The private side of yacht discovery.*

## Simple operating model (3 offers only)

1. **House Accounts** — retainers for owners, brokers, shipyards who want brand + demand done properly  
2. **Launch Capsules** — 90-day campaigns for a yacht, fleet, or charter season (story + film + private outreach)  
3. **The Gallery** — invitation-only digital salon where vetted clients browse and request introductions (our moat)

Everything else is noise. Ship these three.

---

## The creative wedge: Charter → Own flywheel

Charter guests are the warmest future buyers — and almost nobody markets that path deliberately.

**Play:** Partner with 5–10 elite charter houses. Capture post-charter intent (discreetly). Feed warm demand into broker partners and for-sale inventory. Share economics.

That single loop is more valuable than generic “luxury digital ads.”

## 90-day build (no theater)

### Days 1–30 — Credibility
- Lock 3 founding partners (1 brokerage, 1 charter house, 1 shipyard/brand)
- Publish the Gallery manifesto + visual identity
- Hire: 1 producer (film/story) + 1 growth operator (pipeline)

### Days 31–60 — Proof
- Run 2 Launch Capsules end-to-end
- Open Gallery as private beta (invite-only, 100 seats)
- Instrument one metric that matters: **Qualified Introductions / month**

### Days 61–90 — Leverage
- Convert proof into case studies + partner revenue share model
- Soft-close next 10 House Accounts off introductions, not decks
- Define Series Seed / profit path from network effects, not headcount
