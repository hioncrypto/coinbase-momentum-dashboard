## What we collect

- **Account:** email address (for login and support)
- **Payment:** processed by Stripe; we do not store full card numbers
- **Usage:** scanner settings, alert preferences, and technical logs (IP, browser) for security and debugging
- **Notifications:** email address and optional webhook URL you provide for alerts

## How we use data

- Provide and improve the Service
- Send subscription and alert emails you request
- Prevent fraud and abuse
- Comply with legal obligations

## What we do not do

- We do **not** require exchange account passwords
- We do **not** sell your personal information

## Storage and retention

- Settings may be stored in our database and/or session storage on secure servers
- Alert history and deduplication state are kept as long as your account is active
- You may request deletion by contacting support

## Third parties

- **Stripe** — payments  
- **Hosting provider** (e.g. Railway) — application hosting  
- **Exchange APIs** — public market data (Coinbase, Binance, Kraken, KuCoin)  
- **Email provider** — alert delivery  

## Cookies and URLs

The app may store settings in browser session state and URL parameters. We avoid putting sensitive data (passwords, webhook secrets) in URLs where possible.
