Studio thickener (rack)

Not always on the floor board — used to fatten the recorded lead:

  • Eventide H949 Harmonizer — slight detune (~0.989) for width
  • Think of it as a parallel “almost unison” double after the amp/mic chain

Elsewhere on the album

Other tracks also used Boss DS-1 into cleaner Marshall / Roland JC-120 paths. For the Surfing singing lead, prioritize Tube Driver → Marshall over raw DS-1 slam.

Settings mindset

  • Drive: enough sustain to sing — stop before fizzy high-gain
  • Mids forward (~900–1k feel)
  • Presence / top end open for liquid leads
  • Optional short delay / modulation after the board for shimmer

Modeler cheat sheet

  • Amp: Plexi / JCM800-ish, gain mid-low
  • Pre-OD: Tube Driver or “smooth tube OD,” drive ~4–6
  • EQ: scoop less — keep mids for the singing lead
  • FX: 15–30 ms slap or light chorus; micro-pitch ±5–10 cents

DS-1 tip (album-adjacent)

If you only have a Boss DS-1: Distortion high, Tone ~8–9 o’clock, Level to taste — into a cleaner amp so it doesn’t turn to thrash fuzz.
