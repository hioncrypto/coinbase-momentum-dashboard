What each control does here

  • Tube Drive 6 — singing sustain, not metal fizz
  • Hi 8 / Lo 4 — bright lead edge, controlled bottom
  • Marshall Mid 8 / Bass 3 — throaty mid-forward crunch platform
  • H949 0.989 — almost-unison thickener (abrasive width)

Album-adjacent DS-1

Other album tones used Boss DS-1 into cleaner amps. Starting point if that’s your only dirt box:

  • Distortion high (~8–10)
  • Tone ~8–9 o’clock (darker)
  • Level to match clean volume
  • Into a cleaner Marshall / JC-120 — not a already-hot amp
