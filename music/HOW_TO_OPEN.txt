MUSIC TEACHER — standalone project (not part of your crypto tracker)

HOW TO OPEN
-----------
PHONE
  Music Teacher home:
  https://htmlpreview.github.io/?https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hioncrypto/coinbase-momentum-dashboard/cursor/music-teacher-app-0b84/music/index.html

  Interactive Circle of Fifths (tap keys + sound):
  https://htmlpreview.github.io/?https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hioncrypto/coinbase-momentum-dashboard/cursor/music-teacher-app-0b84/music/interactive_circle.html

  Teach — how each analyzed song uses the circle:
  https://htmlpreview.github.io/?https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hioncrypto/coinbase-momentum-dashboard/cursor/music-teacher-app-0b84/music/teach.html

  Pedalboard — Surfing with the Alien (settings printed on each pedal):
  https://htmlpreview.github.io/?https://raw.githubusercontent.com/hioncrypto/coinbase-momentum-dashboard/cursor/music-teacher-app-0b84/music/pedalboard.html?v=2

DESKTOP
  1. Download index.html and songs.js into the same folder
     (or download the whole music/ folder).
  2. Double-click index.html — opens in your browser.
     No install, no server, no crypto app required.

WHAT'S INSIDE
  • Home — Music Teacher
  • Interactive Circle — tap keys, hear notes, relatives, diatonic chords
    Phone: .../music/interactive_circle.html on the same branch
  • Teach — guided circle lessons per song
  • Pedalboard — Surfing with the Alien studio / bedroom schematic
  • Lessons — circle, Aeolian cadence, modes, articulation, 12 lenses
  • Song Lab — pick song + lens + section (Pitch lens shows the wheel)
  • Circle — song sections on the circle-of-fifths map
  • Drills — practice quizzes
