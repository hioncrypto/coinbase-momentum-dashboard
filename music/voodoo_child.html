Wah rocking motion

Heel = darker / more closed filter. Toe = brighter / more open. The intro often starts parked about ⅔ toward toe (nasal), then rocks with each lick.

HEELclosed · dark TOEopen · bright

OPEN ~35% · toward heel

1 · ParkStart ~⅔ open for the nasal intro growl.
2 · Rock outAs the lick rises, push toe (brighter).
3 · Rock backAs the lick falls / chord hits, heel (darker).
4 · Chord stabsOften more open on E7♯9 hits — “wah!” accent.

Muted string chugging + pick motion

The intro’s grit is palm-muted chugs on the low strings — edge of the picking hand rests on the bridge so notes go chuck / chug, not open ring. Alternate ↓ down and ↑ up strokes keep the pulse locked.

Fretting hand · neck location

Stay at the nut end of the neck (frets 0–3). Chugs = open low E. Lick uses 2nd fret on D/G. Bend toward fret 3 on low E. Do not mute with the fretting hand for the chug — that’s the other hand.

Muting hand · where it sits

Place the side / heel of the picking hand lightly on the low strings right next to the bridge saddles — not up on the fretboard. Cover mostly low E (and A if scratching). Keep pressure light: dead “chuck,” not choked silence.

How to hold the mute
1. Side of picking hand rests lightly on the bridge / low strings.
2. Dig the pick DOWN through low E — short “chuck,” not a sustained note.
3. Come UP through the same string for the reply stroke.
4. Keep the wrist loose; motion is mostly forearm + wrist, pick tip pointing slightly in.
5. When the melodic 0h2p0 starts, lift the palm slightly so the notes can ring/wah.
Pick motion rules for this intro
↓ Down on the beat / accent chugs and on the start of each lick cell.
↑ Up on the off-beats — fills the eighth-note groove between downs.
Don’t “pick from the elbow” on every note — small stroke, big attitude.
Fuzz stays on: the mute + pick attack creates the chug; the wah shapes the vowel.
↓ DownstrokePick moves toward the floor through the string. Accents + chugs.
↑ UpstrokePick moves toward the ceiling. Off-beat replies.
PM chugPalm kills sustain — you hear attack, not ring.
ReleaseLift palm when the wah lick sings so notes can open.

Stack the parts
1) Palm-mute chugs with ↓↑ eighths → 2) release mute into wah lick → 3) rock wah heel↔toe → 4) E7♯9 DOWN stabs.
On each 0h2p0 rise → toe + mostly ↓ accents.
On the fall / low E → heel; optional light PM again between phrases.

Solo vocabulary: E minor pentatonic + blues notes, wide bends, double-stops, controlled feedback. Optional Octavia later in leads for the octave scream.
